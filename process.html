My Development Process

From initial discovery to final deployment, I follow a rigorous, data-driven workflow to ensure your e-learning modules achieve their learning objectives.

Phase 1

Discovery & Needs Analysis

Before a single slide is designed, we must understand the core problem. We define the target audience, the learning objectives, and the technical constraints.

  • Stakeholder Interviews
  • Audience Profiling
  • LMS Environment Audit
Deep Dive
Phase 2

Storyboarding & Prototyping

Visualizing the flow of information. We create detailed text storyboards and interactive prototypes to align on content before full-scale development begins.

By sketching custom interface mockups and logical triggers early on, we map out clear user paths and instructional pathways. This proactive design verification guarantees stakeholder alignment and minimizes structural updates during the build phase.

  • Interactive Wireframing & UX Mockups
  • Instructional Flow & Narrative Scripting
Phase 3

Authoring & Development

Bringing the storyboard to life. We integrate premium multimedia assets, program complex branching pathways, and construct responsive SCORM/xAPI packages.

Pixel-Perfect Engineering

Every button click, variable change, and branching path is meticulously coded and tested for responsive performance across all devices and LMS platforms.

  • Custom Interaction Programming
  • LMS SCORM/xAPI Packaging

Final Handoff

Delivery of source files, SCORM packages, and LMS deployment guides.

Phase 4

Quality Assurance & Launch

Rigorous multi-device testing and SCORM Cloud validation ensure zero bugs upon launch. I ensure absolute compatibility across different browsers, mobile devices, and screen resolutions.

Every module undergoes an extensive diagnostic check prior to launch, verifying grading logic, bookmarking consistency, and navigation constraints so that your training is delivered with perfect operational stability.

  • Cross-Browser & Multi-Device Testing
  • LMS Integration & Grading Logic Audits
